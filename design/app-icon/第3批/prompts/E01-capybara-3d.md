The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px. The character's charm comes first: appealing proportions, a big head, a clear expression. Avoid generic stock "mountain and sun" picture icons.

Style: light, clean 3D illustration with matte soft surfaces and a thick white outline around the character, simple gradients, no glossy hotspots, no heavy fur texture.
