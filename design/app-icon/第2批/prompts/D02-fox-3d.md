The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept D02 — a fox curled around its favorite picture, guarding it with its fluffy tail.
Style: light, clean 3D illustration with matte soft surfaces and a thick white outline around the character, simple gradients, no glossy hotspots, no heavy fur texture.
Composition: a fox curled in a circle, filling about 80% of the square, slightly cropped by the bottom-right edge, its big tail wrapping around a picture card.
Expression: sly half-lidded eyes, contented smile.
Functional hook: one big rounded picture card (pink sky, purple mountain) held inside the curl. Background: linear gradient from deep navy at top to indigo at bottom.
