The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept V01 — a little bird whose nest is built from picture cards.
Style: smooth soft-gradient vector illustration, flat shapes with gentle one-hue gradients, no outlines.
Composition: a round chick peeking out of a nest made of five big rounded picture cards stacked in a crisscross pile, the nest fills the lower 60% of the square and is cropped by the bottom edge, the chick is centered above.
Expression: curious upward glance, tiny beak open.
Functional hook: the nest cards in single bold colors (coral, yellow, sky blue, mint, lilac). Chick: soft yellow. Background: linear gradient from deep blue at top to indigo at bottom.
