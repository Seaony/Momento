The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept C01 — a snail carrying its home on its back, and its home is a rolled-up stack of pictures.
Style: soft clay-like 3D with matte surfaces, gentle rounded forms, minimal shading.
Composition: a big snail entering from the bottom-left, about 75% of the square, cropped by the bottom and left edges, its shell is a big spiral.
Expression: calm, slow and happy, eyes on short stalks looking toward the top-right.
Functional hook: the spiral shell is made of a long rounded strip of picture tiles in rainbow order (coral, orange, yellow, mint, sky blue) coiling inward. Background: smooth linear gradient from cream at top to soft peach at bottom.
