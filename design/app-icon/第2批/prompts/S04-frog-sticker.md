The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept S04 — a frog catching a picture from the web with its tongue, the way it catches flies.
Style: bold flat sticker illustration with thick smooth dark outlines, flat fills with only very subtle gradients, a white sticker outline around the character.
Composition: a big round frog head rising from the bottom-left, about 70% of the square, cropped by the bottom and left edges; its long pink tongue arcs up to the top-right.
Expression: focused and delighted, big round eyes looking up at the catch.
Functional hook: the tip of the tongue is stuck to one big rounded picture card (sunset orange with a hill), tilted. Background: fresh lime-to-green linear gradient. Colors: green frog, pink tongue, white, one orange accent.
