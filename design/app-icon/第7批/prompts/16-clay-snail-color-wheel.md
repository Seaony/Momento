The app: Momento, a desktop image reference library where designers collect pictures from the web, browse them in a masonry grid of rounded photo cards, tag them, and filter by an 8-color palette extracted from each image.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.
Brand color: indigo-violet purple (around #6553F0). Purple must be the dominant color of the whole icon. At most three main colors in total. Do not draw a generic "mountain and sun" picture symbol anywhere; photo cards show simple soft color blocks instead.
Expression rule: cute and natural only. Never smug, sly, mischievous, half-lidded, side-eye or evil-looking.
Avoid heavy glossy plastic highlights, inner glow, haze, texture on the background and cluttered small details.

Concept: a snail whose shell is a spiral color wheel, standing for the color palette Momento extracts from every image.
Style: soft clay-like 3D with matte surfaces, simple gradients.
Composition: side view, the snail crawls in from the left edge; the round shell is very large and fills most of the canvas; head and eye stalks lean toward the top-right.
Expression: content little smile, eyes closed happily.
Colors: shell spiral bands in violet, lilac, pink and indigo; body cream; background smooth gradient from indigo-violet #6553F0 (top) to deep violet (bottom).
