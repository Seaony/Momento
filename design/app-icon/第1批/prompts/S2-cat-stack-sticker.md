The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept S2 — a cat sleeping on top of a neat pile of pictures, guarding the collection.
Style: bold flat sticker illustration with thick smooth dark outlines, flat fills, very subtle gradients.
Composition: a big round cat lying across a stack of three large rounded image cards, filling about 80% of the square, the stack cropped by the bottom edge, the cat tail draping over the right edge. Cards are offset like a fanned pile, each a single bold color: coral, teal, yellow.
Expression: one eye open in a lazy, mischievous peek.
Functional hook: the stacked color cards. Background: deep navy blue linear gradient. Cat: cream white with ginger patches.
