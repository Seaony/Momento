The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept S1 — a magpie, the bird famous for collecting things it likes, carrying a picture home.
Style: bold flat sticker illustration with thick smooth dark outlines, flat fills with only a very subtle gradient, white sticker outline around the character.
Composition: the magpie head and chest diagonally entering from the bottom-left, about 70% of the square, cropped by the bottom and left edges, leaning toward the top-right.
Expression: sly half-lidded eyes, proud of its find.
Functional hook: it holds in its beak one large rounded photo card (simple orange sunset over a mountain) tilted at an angle. Background: solid warm sunflower yellow with a gentle linear gradient to slightly deeper yellow. Colors: black, white, yellow, one orange accent.
