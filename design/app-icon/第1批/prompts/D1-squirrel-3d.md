The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept D1 — a squirrel stashing a favorite picture for later, like it stashes acorns.
Style: light, clean 3D illustration with matte soft surfaces and a thick white outline around the character, simple gradients, no glossy hotspots.
Composition: a chubby squirrel peeking in from the right edge, body cropped by the right and bottom edges, about 70% of the square, big fluffy tail curling up behind it.
Expression: curious upward glance, mischievous little grin.
Functional hook: it clutches one big rounded photo card (simple green hills and blue sky) to its chest like a treasure. Background: linear gradient from fresh mint green at top to teal at bottom.
