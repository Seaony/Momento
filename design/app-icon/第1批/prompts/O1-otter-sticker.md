The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept O1 — an otter floating on its back, holding its favorite picture on its belly the way otters hold their favorite stone.
Style: bold flat sticker illustration with thick smooth dark outlines, flat fills with subtle gradients, white sticker outline.
Composition: the otter seen from above, diagonal across the square from bottom-left to top-right, about 80% of the square, feet and tail cropped by the bottom-left corner.
Expression: blissful, eyes closed in happy arcs, a small smile.
Functional hook: on its belly it holds one big rounded photo card (simple pink-orange sunset over a sea) with both paws. Background: clear water, linear gradient from bright aqua at top to deeper blue at bottom, a couple of simple white ripple rings.
