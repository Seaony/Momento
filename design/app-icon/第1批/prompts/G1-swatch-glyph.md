The app: a visual reference library for designers, photographers and creators. It gathers saved images, screenshots, moodboards and photos into one private library on your own device (Mac now, iPhone and iPad later), shows them as a masonry grid of image cards, and sorts them by color, tag and folder.
Output one full-bleed 1:1 square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024, square outer corners, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border. One main subject only. Make it look like a professionally designed, high-quality App Store icon: confident shapes, crisp clean edges, strong contrast, readable at 32px.

Concept G1 — a minimal symbol: image cards fanned out and ordered by color.
Style: minimal glyph on a dark background, flat shapes with a soft inner gradient and a faint glow, no character, no face.
Composition: five large rounded rectangle cards fanned out like a hand of cards from a single pivot at the bottom center, centered, together filling about 65% of the square. Colors in rainbow order from left to right: coral red, orange, yellow, green, blue. Each card has a small simple mountain-and-sun cutout shape so it reads as an image, not a letter.
Functional hook: the color-ordered fan. Background: near-black deep indigo with a subtle linear gradient.
