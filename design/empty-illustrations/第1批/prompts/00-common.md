Common style for every image in this set (Momento: a local image/reference library app for designers):
- One small "empty state" spot illustration, the kind shown in the middle of an empty list in a polished modern app or website.
- Monochrome only: pure black ink (#000000) on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). The only other tones allowed are a few flat light-gray fills (about 8-15% gray) to give soft shape. No other colors, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no noise.
- Clean vector-like line art: uniform medium-weight rounded strokes, rounded caps and joins, simple geometric shapes, generous negative space. Calm, friendly, minimal — not cartoonish, no faces, no characters, no hands.
- Exactly one compact composition, centered, occupying about 55-60% of the canvas width, with plenty of white margin on all sides.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI chrome, no border, no frame.
- 1:1 square, 1024x1024.
- All images in the set must look like one consistent family (same stroke weight, same gray fill tone, same level of detail: 3-6 shapes plus 1-3 tiny accent marks like small sparkles or dots).
